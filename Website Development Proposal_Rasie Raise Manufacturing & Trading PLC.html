Website Development Proposal

Raise Manufacturing & Trading PLC B2B Strategy

1. Executive Summary

For nearly a decade, Raise Manufacturing & Trading PLC has built a formidable reputation in the mechanical and electrical engineering sector, transitioning into a leading industrial equipment manufacturing company. This proposal outlines a comprehensive web development strategy designed to reflect this legacy and your accelerated growth over the past two years. The primary goal is to establish a robust digital presence that clearly communicates your expertise in HVAC systems, showcases both legacy and recent flagship projects, and serves as a powerful B2B lead generation tool, featuring an integrated system for clients to request service quotes directly online. The resulting website will utilize a highly structured, modern visual system to ensure a trustworthy user experience across all devices.

2. Project Scope & Architecture

Based on your foundational 2024 company profile—and specifically designed to accommodate the massive accomplishments and new capabilities added since then—the website will be structured to serve the specific needs of corporate clients, contractors, and industrial partners seeking top-tier electromechanical solutions.

Core Pages:

  • Home: A high-impact landing page establishing Raise's 10-year credibility, emphasizing the transition to industrial manufacturing, and directing users to core services.
  • Projects (Portfolio): A structured showcase of completed work, prominently featuring flagship installations like the Zemen Bank modular chillers and Dire International Hotel Daikin VRF systems.
  • Services & Manufacturing: Detailed breakdowns tailored to technical buyers, including: VRF/VRV Installation, Ventilation Duct Works, Electrical Control Panels, Cold Rooms, and System Design.
  • Quote Request Portal: An interactive, multi-step form allowing potential clients to request customized pricing quotes for specific HVAC services and manufacturing needs directly through the website, streamlining your sales pipeline.
  • About Us: Highlighting your journey, ISO/recognition certificates, workshop capabilities at Bole Brass, and commitment to quality.
  • Contact & Support: Streamlined inquiry forms, direct contact info, and maintenance/support request portals for existing clients.

3. Design & Development Workflow

The project follows a streamlined approach to ensure high efficiency and alignment with strategic brand goals. Visual identity and layouts are applied directly during the interactive development phase using Framer to ensure a highly responsive, performant live site from the start.

Phase Description Key Deliverables
Phase 1: Strategy, Brand Refresh & Architecture Conducting a lightweight modernization of your current brand identity (colors, typography) alongside finalizing sitemaps and technical requirements. Refreshed Brand Mini-Guidelines, Approved Sitemap, Wireframes
Phase 2: Design & Platform Development Building the site natively in Framer, establishing the visual system, and integrating the interactive quote request forms directly within the environment. Functional Beta Website with Quote Portal
Phase 3: Testing & Revisions Rigorous QA testing across devices and client feedback integration. Approved Release Candidate
Phase 4: Launch & Infrastructure Connecting the custom domain and deploying to the live server. Live Website, 1-Year Active Hosting
