1. Executive Summary

Raise Manufacturing & Trading PLC has built a substantial reputation in mechanical and electrical engineering, growing from an installation and service business into an industrial equipment manufacturer with its own fabrication workshop.

Most of that growth is undocumented. Your last company profile is already two years behind the business, and none of it is visible online — so corporate clients, consultants, and contractors shortlisting HVAC partners have no way to verify your current capability before the first meeting. Every contract is won from a standing start.

This proposal sets out a website built specifically to close that gap: a platform that documents your delivered projects, states your manufacturing capability in the technical language your buyers use, and captures qualified enquiries through a structured quote request portal.

2. What the Website Must Achieve

Every decision in this project is measured against four objectives:

  • Pass the vetting stage. When a consultant or procurement officer searches for Raise before issuing a tender, they find evidence of delivered work at the scale they need.
  • Convert credibility into enquiries. A structured quote request portal that captures project type, scale, and technical requirement — so enquiries arrive qualified rather than as a blank email.
  • Document the manufacturing shift. Make clear that Raise now fabricates, not only installs — the capability that separates you from the installation-only competition.
  • Own the search result. Rank for the terms your buyers actually search: industrial HVAC, VRF installation, cold room construction, and ventilation duct works in Ethiopia.

3. Project Scope & Architecture

The structure below is an architecture, not a content list. It is drawn from your 2024 company profile purely as a starting point — that document no longer reflects the business, so the current production, capability, and project information you supply in Week 1 is what will actually populate these pages. The structure is deliberately built to absorb it.

Core Pages

  • Home: A high-impact landing page establishing Raise's track record, emphasising the transition into industrial manufacturing, and directing visitors to the core services.
  • Projects (Portfolio): A structured, expandable showcase of completed work — including earlier flagship installations such as the Zemen Bank modular chillers and the Dire International Hotel Daikin VRF systems, alongside the more recent projects that were never documented. Final selection and project details are confirmed with you in Week 1.
  • Services & Manufacturing: Detailed breakdowns written for technical buyers. The 2024 service list — VRF/VRV installation, ventilation duct works, electrical control panels, cold rooms, kitchen and laundry systems, and system design — is the baseline; any capability added since will be built in at no extra cost, provided it is confirmed during Phase 1.
  • Quote Request Portal: A structured multi-step form allowing clients to request pricing for specific services, routing complete enquiries directly to your inbox.
  • About Us: Company journey, recommendation letters and certificates, and the workshop capability at Bole Brass.
  • Contact & Support: Direct contact details, enquiry form, and a maintenance request route for existing clients.

Included in Every Page

  • Fully responsive layouts, tested on phone, tablet, and desktop
  • On-page SEO setup: page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, image alt text, sitemap
  • A brand identity refresh applied consistently across the site (Phase 1 — line-itemed in Section 6)
  • Two consolidated rounds of revisions during Phase 3
  • Analytics installed so you can see who is visiting and what they request

Not Included in This Quote

Named explicitly so there are no assumptions on either side. Any of these can be quoted separately:

  • Professional photography or video of your sites, installations, and workshop — strongly recommended, see Section 5
  • Amharic translation or a multilingual version of the site
  • Ongoing SEO campaigns, content marketing, or paid advertising
  • E-commerce, online payment processing, or customer login areas
  • Integration with ERP, CRM, or accounting systems
  • A full brand identity rebuild — Phase 1 is a refresh of your existing identity, not a ground-up redesign

5. Timeline & Requirements

The project runs as a structured four-week sprint. The four weeks begin once the deposit is received and the assets listed below are in hand — not on the date of signature. This is the single biggest factor in hitting the launch date.

Week 1

Discovery, Brand Refresh & Asset Gathering

Finalising the sitemap, refreshing the visual identity, and gathering assets — with a heavy focus on documenting your 2024–2026 projects and new manufacturing capability.

Weeks 2 – 3

Framer Development & Content Integration

Building the platform, writing and placing the technical service copy, and structuring the full project portfolio.

Week 4

QA Testing, Revisions & Launch

Cross-device testing, both revision rounds, domain connection, and public launch.

What I Need From Raise

A four-week timeline only holds if these arrive on schedule. Delays here move the launch date by the same number of days.

  • One named decision-maker with authority to give final approval, available for a weekly check-in.
  • Project photography — the best available images of Zemen Bank, Dire International Hotel, the Bole Brass workshop, and your teams on site. Delivered in Week 1. If suitable photography does not exist, tell me early: this is the one gap that materially limits how good the site can look, and I can quote a shoot separately.
  • Brand files — your logo in vector format (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF), if available.
  • Current production & capability information. Your 2024 profile is the only written record I have, and it is two years out of date. This does not need to be a polished document — a plain list is enough: what you now manufacture in-house, your current service lines, and the projects completed since 2024. I will write it into publishable copy. This is the single most important input in the project; without it the site documents the company you were, not the one you are.
  • Technical source material — equipment specifications, project details, certificates, and recommendation letters.
  • Consolidated feedback — one combined response per round, within three working days.
  • Domain access — registrar login for your existing domain, or authority to register a new one on your behalf.
